Questions

    
Before you order.

    
Still unsure? Ask us on WhatsApp and we reply with the details for your device.

    Ask a question ↗
  

  

    
Do you have devices in stock?
No. We source each device after you request it, so availability and price are confirmed per request.

    
When do I pay?
Only after you have seen the unit and agreed the final price, delivery fee and warranty terms. We confirm the payment steps with you on WhatsApp before purchase.

    
What does the 7-day warranty cover?
A 7-day checking period from delivery, subject to conditions. The exact conditions for your device are sent to you in writing before you confirm.

    
Who pays for delivery?
The customer. We tell you the delivery fee for your area before you confirm the order.

    
Which areas do you cover?
We are based in Ajman and deliver to Ajman, Sharjah, Dubai and Umm Al Quwain. Ask us about other emirates.

    
Can I see the device before buying?
Yes. We send photos or a video of the actual unit, including the details you ask for, before you confirm.
